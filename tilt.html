Fixed Tilt Box Design

This page features a box tilted at a 45-degree angle on the right side. Unlike the interactive tilt box that responds to mouse movement, this box has a fixed rotation that adds visual interest to the page layout.

The tilted element creates a dynamic design that breaks from traditional rectangular layouts, drawing attention to important content or calls to action.

This design approach works well for showcasing featured products, special promotions, or important notifications in a visually striking way.
